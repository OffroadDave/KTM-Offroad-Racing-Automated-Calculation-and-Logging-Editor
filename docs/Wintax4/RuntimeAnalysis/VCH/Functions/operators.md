### Arithmetic operators

####  - (expression1 \- expression2)

Subtraction

The result of the subtraction (–) operator is the difference between the operands. 

####  * (expression1 * expression2)

Multiplication

The result of the (*) operator is the multiplication between the factor. 

####  / (expression1 / expression2)

Division

The result of the (/) operator is the division between the factor.

####  ^ (number ^ expression2)

Exponent

Raises a number to the power of an exponent.

####  \+ (expression1 + expression2)

Addition

The result of the addition (+) operator is the sum of the operands.
