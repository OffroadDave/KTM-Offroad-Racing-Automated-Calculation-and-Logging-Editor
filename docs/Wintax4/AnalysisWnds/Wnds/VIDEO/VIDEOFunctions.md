### Functions

The [Media window](VIDEOFeatures.md) has the following functions:

  * Connect Cursor

  * Zoom

  * Comparison




####  Connect Cursor

When, in the Setup/General, the Auto Connect check is enabled and the PTS (Presentation Time Stamp) Channel is configured, the video is connected from and to the graphic windows. In fact, when the cursor is moved on a [Graph](../GRA/GRAWndOverview.md) window, the Media window shows the frame at the time selected by the cursor. In the same way, by playing video, the cursor resets on the other windows according to the moment of time associated with the frame currently showing in the video window.

####  Zoom

The Multimedia window is not linked to the Zoom of the other windows. The only way to zoom an area of the tracks is to use the Zoom In and Zoom Out commands.

####  Comparison

The Media window permits comparisons. Each video is synchronized according to its PTS channel like in the picture below. The first lap that is loaded will define the maximum time range of analysis.
