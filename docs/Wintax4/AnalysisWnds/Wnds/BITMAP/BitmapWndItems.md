### Window Elements

The Bitmap window is composed by a graphic area on a background.

#### Graphic Area

The graphic area of the window shows the selected image. The image could be stretched or not stretched, depends on parameters chosen in configuration. If the image is not stretched the window could show only a section of the picture if the window is smaller than the image or the background if the window is greater.
