### Window Elements

The Gauge window is formed by two parts: a graphic area and an information area

#### Graphic Area

The graphic area of the window shows the gauge display of the configured channels. The single graph displays a scale of values with the corresponding references step and the indicators of the minimum and maximum value.

#### Info Area

Each graph corresponds to an information area that can be found below the graph itself in case of vertical layout or, on the right of the graphs in case of horizontal layout. The info area displays some parameters of the channel: in one box there is the name, in the other boxes, clockwise, are the current values. The maximum and minimum values (min and max in case of post processing analysis) are calculated from the beginning of the lap to the current position of the cursor).
