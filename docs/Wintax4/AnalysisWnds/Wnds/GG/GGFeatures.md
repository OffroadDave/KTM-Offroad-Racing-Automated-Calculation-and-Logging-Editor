### G-G Diagram Window Overview

The G-G Diagram Window can describe the performance of a vehicle. It is called G-G Diagram because it plots lateral acceleration or lateral G against longitudinal acceleration or longitudinal G, where G refers to G force and 1g equals 9.8 m/s2 of acceleration. The G-G Diagram Window is constructed using lateral and longitudinal accelerations recorded during a lap. A vehicle travelling at high speed has a great many forces acting on it, due to acceleration, braking (negative acceleration) and lateral acceleration. Acceleration is therefore a very useful parameters to use, managing to tie together speed with the forces which may be acting on a vehicle at any time.

The G-G Diagram Window works in both way, Post Processing and Real Time.
