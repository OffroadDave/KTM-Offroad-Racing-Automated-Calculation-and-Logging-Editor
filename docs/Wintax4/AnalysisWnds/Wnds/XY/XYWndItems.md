### Window Elements

#### Graphic Area

The graphic area displays the cursor and the grid of the windows, the graphic elements of the channels (grids, cursors, markers, corners and cursors of the Best Fit analysis).

The Graphic Area can also have more than one X axis, as you can see in [XY configuration](XYWndSetup.md); the effect is shown in the picture below.

According to the configured graphic mode, the channels values (Graph Mode) or the frequency distribution of the channels remarks are displayed (Smooth Mode and Percent Mode). In the following picture you can see an XY window in Smooth and Percent mode.

In Smooth and Percent mode only the first configured Y channel is managed; all other channels are ignored.

#### Info Channel Area

The Info Channel Area displays the names of the channels, the values corresponding to the present positions of the cursor, the additional information (ex.: Best Fit interpolation degree). The groups of info boxes of a channel are alternatively arranged on the right and on the left of the graphic area above the respective scale. 

#### Y Scale Area

The Y Scale Area displays the Y scales of the channels. The Y scales are alternatively arranged on the right and on the left of the graphic area. At the bottom of Y scale it is possible to display the measurement unit of the channel, if configured in Channel Parameters and in XY properties. The text of the scales can be displayed either vertically or horizontally.

#### X Scale Area

The X Scale Area displays the X scale. The window can have one to four X scales.

#### Zoom bars

Horizontal Zoom Bar: displays the percentage of horizontal zoom in the window, allows moving the horizontal zooming area.

Vertical Zoom Bar: displays the percentage of vertical zoom in the window, allows moving the vertical zooming area.

#### Legend bar

When Scale Mode is configured on Legend, a legend bar appears; it contains the name of the channels ordered by column.
