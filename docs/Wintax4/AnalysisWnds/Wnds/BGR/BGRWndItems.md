### Window Elements

The Bargraph window is formed by two sections: a graphic area and an information area.

#### Graphic area

The graphic area of the Bargraph window shows the bar graphs of the configured channels. Each graph displays a scale of values with the corresponding references and the indicator of the minimum and maximum values. When a channel is selected the window shows that channel highlighted; the selection is always for a single channel.

#### Information Area

Each graph has an information area that is placed below the graph itself with a vertical layout, while it is placed on the right of the graph with a horizontal layout. The information area shows some parameters of the channel: the name in the box on the top, and then in clockwise direction are appearing: current value, maximum value, minimum value (Min and Max in case of post processing analysis are calculated from the beginning of the lap to the current position of the cursor).
