### Window Elements

The Steering Wheel window is formed by two parts: a graphic area and an information area

#### Graphic Area

The graphic area of the window shows the steering wheel of the configured channel. Around the steering wheel some scale indicators are shown. The ticks are related to the Maximum and Minimum values configured for the channel.

#### Information Area

The graph has a corresponding information area that can be found below the graph itself. This info area displays the name and the values of the channel at the current position.
