### Steering Wheel Window Overview

The Steering Wheel window displays through a graphic wheel the value of the channels samples at the instant of the Real Time acquisition or at the current position of the cursor in the post processing analysis. The window could show only one channel at time and works with single lap or with compare of two laps. If more than two laps are loaded for comparison, the Steering Wheel window will analyze the first two enabled datasets.
