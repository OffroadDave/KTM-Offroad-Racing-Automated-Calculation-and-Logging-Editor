### Elements of the window

####  Title

The title is editable in configuration and is displayed on the caption.

####  Area of the text

The text is edited directly on the window and the length can not exceed 4000 characters.

The text is automatically placed on the window according to the configured font and the size of the window. If the text does not have enough space to be displayed, a vertical scroll bar appears.
